02  /  COMPARISON

Which direction first?

DIRECTION A

Forward deployment

Daily question Can a user finish a real task with the system?

Starting evidence At IT Vortex, I diagnosed client tickets and automated manual workflows. I can explain how a user’s description changes what I investigate.

Trade-off More direct user contact, with customer schedules and travel in the OpenAI example.

DIRECTION B

Applied ML

Daily question When does the model fail, and what does that cost?

Starting evidence Themis connected data cleaning to sentiment classification. Audible emphasizes scientific modeling, while Capital One emphasizes production reliability.

Trade-off More depth in evaluation and pipelines. The two employers ask for different degrees and experience.

PROVISIONAL CHOICE

Test customer-facing deployment first.

My support work gives me a concrete workflow to investigate, and Themis gives me a model I can put in front of a reader. My near-term goal is to demonstrate that I can deliver a small tool and explain what changed for its user.

Assumption Three experienced roles can suggest skills worth practicing. They cannot establish entry-level hiring expectations across a field.

Next experiment Give a classmate a public sample of product reviews and ask them to identify a recurring complaint using Themis. Record where they hesitate, change one thing and repeat the same task. If classification errors dominate, prioritize the applied-ML evaluation instead.
